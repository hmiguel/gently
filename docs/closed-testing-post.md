# Finding closed-test testers

Post in **r/AndroidClosedTesting** or **r/TestersCommunity** once the closed test exists in Play
Console. Then test the apps of the people who answer, and stay opted in to theirs for 14 days as well.

Strangers can only join if their account is on the tester list, so the test uses a Google Group
(groups.google.com, "Anyone can join") added under Closed testing → Testers. Replace `[GROUP LINK]`
with the group's URL, e.g. https://groups.google.com/g/gently-testers.

- Opt-in (web): https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.lixo.gently
- Store page (works for testers after joining): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lixo.gently

**Title:** [Test for test] Gently: call blocker with simple rules (Android 10+)

**Body:**

Hi! I need testers for my first app and I'll happily test yours back. Comment with your link and
I'll join and stay in for the full 14 days.

**Gently** blocks the calls you choose, outgoing, incoming or both, behind an access code:

- Rules like "Block all international calls" or "Allow calls from Mom"
- Pick numbers from contacts, or block hidden numbers
- No ads, no account, no internet permission

How to join (with the Google account you use on your phone):
1. Join the group: [GROUP LINK]
2. Opt in: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.lixo.gently → "Become a tester"
3. Install: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lixo.gently

Please stay opted in for 14 days (you can uninstall the app, just don't leave the test).

Feedback and bug reports very welcome. Thanks! 🙏
